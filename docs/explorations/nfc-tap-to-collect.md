# Exploration — NFC tap-to-collect at the counter («Зернятко по дотику»)

> **Status: exploration, not a decision.** Brainstorm from 2026-08-07 on a
> customer-initiated earn path: a passive NFC tag in a branded puck at the counter, tapped by the Customer's phone to claim the Зернятко. This is the
> NFC twin of issue #61 (flipped QR direction) — same trust model, cheaper
> hardware, stronger token. Filed as issue #179; sibling ideas from the same
> brainstorm are #180 (birthday reward) and #181 (single-use printed codes).
> Related: ADR 0006 (dynamic QR), ADR 0013 (Barista Roster + Shift),
> `docs/explorations/hardware-qr-scanner.md` (the powered-hardware branch of
> the same counter-experience question).

## The proposal

A small branded puck sits by the register. The Customer opens Кавця, taps
«Отримати Зернятко», and holds their phone on the puck; the app reads the NFC
tag, the API verifies the tap and credits the Зернятко with the usual haptic +
success moment. The barista never touches a device — earning becomes
self-service while Redemption stays staff-confirmed.

## TL;DR verdict

Worth keeping on the backlog (#179), gated exactly like #61: it deliberately
loosens the ADR 0006 trust model (a Customer can tap without buying), so it
needs a decided #56, real pilot data, and a Platform-tunable rate-limit design
before promotion. What this exploration adds over #61 is the hardware finding:

1. **The NFC point is not electronics.** It is a passive NFC tag
   in plastic — no battery, no display, no connectivity. The phone's field
   powers the chip; validation rides the Customer's own connection. BOM per
   Café is ~$3–5 landed, cheap enough to ship free with onboarding.
2. **The token can still rotate.** NTAG 424 DNA chips (SUN/SDM) emit a unique
   URL on every read — tag UID + monotonic tap counter + AES-128 CMAC,
   verified server-side. That is a rotating, single-use, replay-proof token
   *generated by the tag chip itself*: the same class of guarantee as ADR
   0006's rotating QR, with none of #61's powered-display hardware (~$140+).
3. **Static tags are disqualified.** NTAG213-class tags (~$0.10–0.30) present
   a fixed payload any phone can clone in seconds; the entire control would
   collapse onto server heuristics. NTAG 424 or nothing — server-side
   authorization is the floor, per the product principles.

## How a tap actually works

1. Customer opens the app and starts the tap flow (foreground, deliberate —
   the app owns the moment, states, and errors).
2. Phone energizes the tag; the tag returns its NDEF payload. On NTAG 424 the
   payload is the SUN URL with fresh counter + CMAC.
3. App sends the payload to the API. The server checks: tag belongs to this
   Café, CMAC valid under the tag's AES key, counter strictly greater than the
   last seen (kills replay and clones), rate limit not exhausted.
4. Server writes the same earn-ledger event as a QR scan (ADR 0010); app
   celebrates.

Requires the phone online — same stance as the rest of the loop (ADR 0006's
offline note carries over unchanged; the *café* needs no connectivity at all,
which beats every powered-counter option in the hardware-scanner exploration).

## Trust model — what is given up, and the caps

Today a human attests "a Purchase happened". An NFC tap attests only "this
Customer was physically at the counter just now". The gap is the free-tap
freeloader, capped by:

- **Rate limit: 1 Зернятко per Customer per Café per Kyiv business day.** The
  business-day primitive from #112 is exactly this key. Platform-tunable per
  the #61 prerequisite.
- **Counter placement** — the tag sits in the barista's line of sight; social
  friction does real work (it is the whole control behind paper stamp cards).
- **Cryptographic presence** — SUN means no remote or replayed taps; the
  freeloader must actually walk in, every time.
- **Earning only.** Redemption keeps its staff confirmation; the blast radius
  is one drink's worth of Зернятко per business day, not a Reward.

Net: roughly the paper-stamp-card trust level, taken deliberately or not at
all. Identical conclusion to #61 — which is why the two share gates and
whichever runs first owns the rate-limit design.

## Fit with the stack

- **Mobile**: `react-native-nfc-manager` ships its own Expo config plugin
  (iOS Core NFC entitlement, Android permission). Dev-build only — fine, we
  are already off Expo Go. Foreground scan works iPhone 7+ and effectively all
  Android NFC phones; Android NFC penetration in Ukraine is high on the back
  of contactless-payment adoption. iOS *background* tag reading (tap with the
  app closed → notification → deep link) exists on iPhone XS/SE2+ as a later
  polish, not the core flow.
- **API**: one endpoint. SUN CMAC verification is NXP-documented with existing
  libraries; per-tag keys derive from a master at provisioning time. Rate
  limit + ledger write reuse existing primitives (#112, ADR 0010).
- **Provisioning**: each tag is written once (URL template + keys) before
  shipping — a script and a phone, not a fleet system. No device registry, no
  OTA, none of the headless-bridge ops the hardware-scanner exploration warned
  about.
- **Accessibility**: a fixed puck has fixed reach, and some phones/cases fail
  NFC. The puck face carries a printed QR fallback, and the staff-scanned path
  remains for everyone — NFC is an accelerator, never the only way (same
  posture as the POS adapter, ADR 0012).

## Cost model

| Item | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| NTAG 424 DNA tag (SUN-capable) | ~€0.60–2.00 |
| Static NTAG213 sticker *(disqualified — clonable)* | ~$0.10–0.30 |
| Printed/acrylic branded puck | ~$1–3 |
| **Landed per Café** | **~$3–5** |

Against the sibling exploration's counter builds (tablet-only ~$140–200,
scanner builds $180–650), the NFC puck is the cheapest possible branded counter
presence — cheap enough to be a free onboarding gift and an acquisition
surface (the puck advertises Кавця to everyone in line, and can carry the
join-QR). Software cost is the real spend: NFC flow + entitlements in the app,
SUN verification + provisioning script + rate limit on the API — a small
slice, but a real one, and worthless before pilot data says self-service
earning is wanted.

## Honest ledger

Pros:

- Rush-hour flow: no staff device, no queue interruption; the barista's hands
  stay on the machine.
- Strongest proof-of-presence of any earn path we've considered — better than
  a scanned code, courtesy of SUN.
- Hardware so cheap it inverts the "no hardware" wedge into "hardware so
  trivial we give it away".
- Demo appeal: «прикладіть телефон — Зернятко ваше» sells the product to a
  CafeOwner in one gesture.

Cons:

- Deliberate trust-model loosening; needs the same promotion gates as #61.
- Two flows to design and support (tap + scan), against "simple = fewest
  concepts" — the tap must read as *the same action*, not a second system.
- NFC edge cases: thick cases, disabled NFC, older iPhones — every failure
  needs a designed fallback into the existing path.
- Key management is new real surface (master key custody, per-tag derivation,
  revocation when a tag walks off a counter).
- Sourcing NTAG 424 stock into Ukraine at small volumes is unverified.

## Sibling ideas from the same brainstorm

- **Birthday reward (#180)** — optional day+month birthday, one scheduled
  push, a birthday Ворожка moment. Table-stakes retention mechanic; per-Café
  Зернятка (ADR 0001) push the bonus variant Pro-side, so v1 is the free
  platform-level delight.
- **Single-use printed codes (#181)** — batch-generated bearer codes on cup
  sleeves / delivery bags; the only earn path that works with zero café
  presence (Glovo/Bolt Food). Weakest trust of the three; shares the same
  rate-limit design.
- **Lapsed-customer nudges** — considered and *not* filed: already on the
  roadmap as churn alerts + AI win-back (the Pro hero, PROJECT_BRIEF «Later»).
  No new issue, no duplicate home.

## Staged recommendation

1. **Now**: nothing to build. #179/#180/#181 sit in Backlog; the brief's
   Backlog section names them.
2. **When #56 is decided and pilots run**: collect the two signals that gate
   #61 and #179 alike — how often is the counter unattended, and do Customers
   actually want self-service earning. Order a handful of NTAG 424 tags then
   (days, not a project) if the signal is positive.
3. **If promoted**: build tap-earning as one slice (app flow + SUN endpoint +
   rate limit + provisioning script), puck production as a checklist item in
   café onboarding, and fold the rate-limit design back into #61 so the two
   experiments stay one concept.
