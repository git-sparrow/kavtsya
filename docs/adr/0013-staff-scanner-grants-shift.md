# Staff scan access via a café-scoped Barista Roster + printed poster (Shift / «Зміна»)

Decided 2026-07-08 (issue #56); **entry mechanism revised 2026-07-12** (grilling session — supersedes the #80 invite handshake). Baristas scan Customers through a café-scoped, revocable scanner capability attached to the barista's **own** Customer account — never the CafeOwner's login, never a separate staff account type. The capability now has two levels: a persistent **Barista Roster** (the CafeOwner approves an account onto a Café's trusted-barista list once) and a per-day **Shift** («Зміна») — a rostered barista scans the Café's **printed wall poster** to activate **Scanner Mode** (scan + Redemption confirm only, no config, no analytics) until the barista, the owner, or a rolling auto-expire cap ends it. This preserves ADR 0003's one-account symmetry: like CafeOwner Mode, a Shift is just a mode on a normal Customer account.

**Why the change.** #80 first shipped an **owner-shown dynamic invite handshake**: the owner generated a per-barista invite QR / short code on their own phone, one invite admitting one barista, a fresh invite per shift. It was secure but made the owner do work *every day*, fighting the project's "safe, easy, enjoyable for every role" goal. The Roster + poster model moves the owner's effort to **exactly twice per barista's lifetime** — approve once (hire), remove once (quit) — and makes daily shift-start a zero-touch wall scan.

**Security rests on the roster, not the poster.** The printed QR is deliberately *not* a secret — power comes from roster membership. A leaked photo scanned by a stranger only ever raises a **pending approval request** that grants nothing until the owner approves, closing the fraud vector a static shared secret would open (a leaked poster + one accomplice minting each other Зернятка and confirming Redemptions).

Considered and rejected: **sharing the owner's login** (exposes personal + management powers, nothing self-heals when staff leave); **dedicated scanner hardware / kiosk-first** (a hardware/provisioning business, breaks the "no POS, no IT" wedge — the kiosk «Стійка» is kept as a later reuse of the same primitive); **flipping the QR direction** (changes ADR 0006's trust model — backlog #61); **per-barista PINs on a shared device** (v2–v3 staff attribution — backlog #62); the **owner-shown dynamic invite** (above — daily owner toil); a **scan-and-go poster with reactive revoke** (fraud already happened inside the window); and an explicit **denylist** for removed baristas (a fourth state for no security gain — "the owner never approves" already covers a fired barista's re-scan).

Consequences:

- **Two concepts, two lifetimes.** A persistent café-scoped roster relationship (approved/removed by the owner) plus a transient per-day shift. The #80 invite path (and its `cafe_shift_invites` handshake) is superseded; the purchase/redemption routes still accept the owner **or** an account with an active shift at that Café.
- **Non-secret poster** = a stable café-scoped join code printed on the wall; scanning it checks the roster.
- **Owner-approval gate.** A non-rostered scan raises a **deduped, rate-limited** pending request, surfaced to the owner as an **operational push + a badge on the roster board** — explicitly *not* gated by the customer-marketing push consent of #24. States are just rostered / pending / none.
- **Instant termination.** Removing a barista ends any active shift immediately; every scan is a server call that re-checks "rostered + active shift at this Café", so revocation needs no client polling.
- **One active Shift per account**, with an explicit switch prompt if a barista scans a second Café's poster while on shift.
- **Shift ends** by the barista («Завершити зміну»), the owner (board or removal), or a **rolling ~16h auto-expire** safety net (café-hours-agnostic — a backstop, not a scheduler).
- **Scanner Mode is a near-kiosk:** scanner + end-shift only; showing one's own QR or signing out requires ending the shift first.
- Shift stays **Free** tier (ADR 0011); `purchases.issued_by_user_id` remains the audit trail.
- The **scanner ≠ scanned** self-farm guard is unchanged and additive to ADR 0003's owner-cannot-earn-at-own-Café rule; barista-pair collusion stays an accepted v1 risk, made visible via `issued_by_user_id` rather than prevented.
